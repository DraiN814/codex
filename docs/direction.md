# Agreed Direction

Source material: user-supplied `Roguelike_Design_Condensed.docx`, September 25, 2026, and the two character reference images. Treat numerical balance as provisional.

## Art and Camera

- Two adult siblings, not four characters: white-haired necromancer and black-haired scythe wielder.
- Style 4, dark comic: detailed ink texture, costume construction, metalwork, expressive faces for menu illustrations.
- Style 7, flat illustration: clear silhouettes, simplified ornament, recognizable weapons and matching colors for gameplay.
- Crimson accent for the brother, lilac for the sister; black clothing and bone-colored ornament shared by both.
- Slightly angled overhead 2D camera, retaining face and costume visibility.
- Co-op camera centers between players, zooms out modestly, and limits separation.
- Dark fairy tale without an oppressive black screen. Forest, heroes, and warning effects must remain readable.

## Portal

- Three-second cast.
- Incoming damage interrupts and resets the cast.
- Both living co-op players confirm before casting. A lone survivor can leave.
- The prototype allows movement while casting; movement cancellation was not agreed.

## First Milestone

Character study sheet and playable forest combat slice. Verify movement, hero identities, telegraphs, camera, and portal behavior before building the permanent economy.

## Next Milestones

1. Review the generated sheet and gameplay atlas, then add consistent directional walk and attack animation assets.
2. Tune solo and co-op combat, enemy pacing, and ability feedback through playtesting.
3. Expand the perk pools and add the specified rarity distribution.
4. Add three save slots, permanent gold, upgrades, export/import, and alternative abilities.

All in-game interface strings remain Russian; project discussion is English.
